Terminology Correction Note:
----------------------------
- "Primary Samples": Primary camera rays cast (Pixels * SPP).
- "Pixels / sec": Image rendering rate (Pixels / Render Time).
- Total ray count including secondary bounces was not explicitly counted in Milestone 3 binaries; total ray tracking is introduced in Milestone 4 diagnostic counters.

Analysis & Cost Observations:
------------------------------
1. **Recursion Depth Scaling (Max Depth 1 vs 5 vs 10)**:
   - At `max-depth = 1` (primary rays only), render time is 77.73 ms. Specular reflection and refraction secondary bounces are terminated at depth 1.
   - Increasing `max-depth` to 5 increases render time to 159.46 ms (~2.05x cost) as reflective and refractive rays bounce recursively through glass and metal surfaces.
   - Increasing `max-depth` from 5 to 10 adds minimal additional cost (163.64 ms vs 159.46 ms, +2.6%), as most secondary rays escape the scene into the sky background within 3 to 5 bounces.

2. **Combined AA and Recursion Scaling**:
   - 16 SPP at max-depth 10 evaluates 1,440,000 primary samples plus millions of secondary reflection/refraction bounces, taking 2732.76 ms.
