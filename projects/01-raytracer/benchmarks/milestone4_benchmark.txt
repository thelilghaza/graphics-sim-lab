Analysis & Cost Observations:
------------------------------
1. **Thin-Lens DOF Overhead vs Pinhole Camera**:
   - Pinhole camera (`aperture = 0.0`) renders 16 SPP demo scene in 5,856.59 ms.
   - Thin-lens camera (`aperture = 0.2`) renders the same scene in 6,036.44 ms (~3.07% overhead).
   - Random lens disk sampling adds minimal compute cost while generating realistic focal plane blur.

2. **Procedural Random Scene Scale (50+ Spheres)**:
   - Increasing scene complexity from 5 spheres to 50+ spheres increases render time from 6.03 s to 42.82 s (~7.09x cost) due to linear $O(N)$ object traversal for 5,064,630 total traced rays.
   - This baseline highlights the critical necessity of spatial acceleration structures (BVH) for Milestone 5.
