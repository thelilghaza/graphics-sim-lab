Terminology Correction Note:
----------------------------
In earlier records, primary sample throughput was broadly summarized as "rays/sec". To maintain strict technical accuracy:
- "Primary Samples" denotes primary camera rays cast (Pixels * SPP).
- "Pixels/sec" denotes final pixel output throughput (Pixels / Time).
- Total rays evaluated (including shadow rays) equal ~2x primary samples for unoccluded surface hits.

Analysis & Cost Observations:
------------------------------
1. **Shading & Shadow Ray Cost vs Milestone 1 Baseline**:
   - Milestone 1 (normal visualization, 1 SPP, 0 shadow rays): 33.93 ms.
   - Milestone 2 (Lambertian shading + shadow rays, 1 SPP): 65.94 ms (~1.94x increase).
   - The addition of shadow ray visibility testing doubles ray-scene intersection tests for unshadowed surface hits.

2. **Anti-Aliasing Scaling**:
   - Execution time scales linearly with sample count $S$ (\(O(S)\)).
   - 4 SPP requires 3.68x the execution time of 1 SPP.
   - 16 SPP requires 14.38x the execution time of 1 SPP.
